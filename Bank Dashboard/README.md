## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing customer data of a retail banking institution.

The dashboard focuses on customer behavior, churn risk, demographics, and financial indicators to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Key Metrics Tracked

* **Total Customers**
* **Churn Rate**
* **Active Customers**
* **Average Customer Tenure**
* Credit Card Holding Distribution
* Credit Score by Age
* Estimated Salary by Age
* Active Customers by Location
* Card Holders by Gender

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

* Power BI Desktop
* Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* DAX (Measures & KPIs)
* Data Modeling

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset includes:

* Customer ID
* Age
* Gender
* Geography (Germany, France, Spain)
* Credit Score
* Tenure
* Balance
* Estimated Salary
* Credit Card Holder (Yes/No)
* Active Member Status
* Churn Status

* Average Tenure

### 🔹 Customer Behavior Insights

* Pie Chart – Customers Holding Credit Cards
* Bar Chart – Card Holders by Gender

### 🔹 Financial & Demographic Insights

* Line/Column Chart – Credit Score by Age
* Line/Column Chart – Estimated Salary by Age

---

## 📈 Business Insights Generated

* Identified churn percentage across the customer base.
* Compared customer engagement across Germany, France, and Spain.
* Observed relationship between age and credit score.
* Analyzed salary distribution patterns by age group.
* Evaluated gender distribution among credit card holders.
* Measured proportion of active vs inactive customers.
