## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing sales performance for **Maven Toys**, a retail toy store chain.

The objective is to monitor revenue, profitability, product performance, and store-level trends to support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Key Metrics Tracked

* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Orders**
* **Total Units Sold**
* Profit Margin (%)
* Average Order Value

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

* Power BI Desktop
* Power Query (ETL & Data Cleaning)
* DAX (KPIs & Calculated Measures)
* Data Modeling & Relationships

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset includes:

* Order Date
* Store Location
* Product Name
* Product Category
* Units Sold
* Revenue
* Cost
* Profit

The data simulates multi-store retail toy sales operations.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Section

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Profit Margin

### 🔹 Sales Analysis

* Revenue Trend by Month
* Revenue by Store Location
* Revenue by Product Category
* Top 10 Best-Selling Products

### 🔹 Profitability Insights

* Profit by Category
* Profit by Store

### 🔹 Interactive Filters

* Date
* Store Location
* Product Category

---

## 📈 Business Insights Generated

* Identified top-performing stores and underperforming branches.
* Analyzed seasonal sales trends.
* Determined high-margin vs low-margin products.
* Evaluated category-wise revenue contribution.
* Detected products generating high revenue but low profitability.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open in Power BI Desktop.
3. Interact with slicers to analyze different stores, categories, and time periods.
