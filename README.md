# Business-Analysis-Visuals

## 📌 Repository Overview

This repository contains multiple end-to-end **Power BI dashboard projects** focused on transforming raw data into actionable business insights.

Each project includes:

* Data Cleaning using Pandas/NumPy or Excel
* Data modeling with relationships
* Code based KPI calculations
* Interactive and dynamic visualizations
* Business insight extraction

The goal of this repository is to demonstrate practical BI skills across different industries.

---

# 📁 Projects Included

---

## 1️⃣ American Super Store – Sales Analysis

**Focus:** Retail performance & profitability

**Key Metrics:**

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Regional Performance
* Category & Sub-Category Analysis
* Discount Impact on Profit

**Insights Generated:**

* Identified loss-making products due to excessive discounts
* Compared regional revenue contribution
* Tracked monthly sales trends

---

## 2️⃣ Bank Customer Analytics Dashboard

**Focus:** Customer behavior & churn analysis

**Key Metrics:**

* Total Customers
* Churn Rate
* Active Customers
* Average Tenure
* Credit Score by Age
* Estimated Salary by Age
* Active Customers by Location (Germany, France, Spain)
* Card Holder Distribution by Gender

**Insights Generated:**

* Measured churn percentage
* Compared customer engagement by geography
* Analyzed demographic impact on financial metrics

---

## 3️⃣ Maven Toys – Sales Analysis

**Focus:** Multi-store retail sales & profitability

**Key Metrics:**

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Units Sold
* Profit Margin
* Sales Trend Over Time
* Store-Level Performance

**Insights Generated:**

* Identified top-performing stores
* Analyzed seasonal sales trends
* Compared product-level profitability
